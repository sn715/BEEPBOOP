the sound

each recording saves to the memory card as its own show, with the date and venue attached. you won't be scrolling through a camera roll to find it.

the button

one button, and nothing to look at.

press it once when the lights go down and once when it's over. there's no screen, and the button is big enough to find by feel in the dark, so you never look away from the stage.

it clips to a bag strap or a belt loop and stays put all night. your hands are free, and your phone keeps its battery for the ride home.

the card

your recordings live on a card you can hold.

no account, no cloud, no subscription.

everything is saved to a memory card inside the device, and nothing disappears if we do. pull the card out and the files open on any computer.

the device works completely on its own. the app is a bonus, never a requirement.

the app, if you want it

every show gets its own page.

open the app and each night has its own entry, with the date, the venue, and any photos or notes you add. after a year it reads like a diary of everywhere you went.

you can also make mixtapes that put your concert recordings next to your own music, then send them back to the device to play anywhere.
